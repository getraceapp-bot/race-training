Step 3 — How to Do a Transaction

Follow these 4 steps every time a customer comes

🚗

Car arrives → Ask for plate number

The customer tells you their plate number. You type it in the app. Example:

21 A 77079

Then tap the 🔍 orange search button

✅

Green box appears = Customer found ✅

If you see green — the customer is in RACE. You can proceed.

✅
Customer Found!
Plate: 21 A 77079 — Ready to process
❌
Red box = Not found
Tell customer to register at race.iq first
⛽

Select fuel type → Enter amount

Tap the fuel type (Normal, Mohasn, Super, Gas). Tap the amount or type it. Tap Add Item.

🎉

Confirm → Transaction done!

Check the plate and amount are correct. Tap Confirm Transaction. You will see a green success screen and your commission is added to your wallet automatically.
